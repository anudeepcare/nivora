# AURYN V9.9.9.25 — Investment Roadmap

Adds the approved long-term investment decision layer without replacing daily execution or V24.2 Market Truth.

## Four decision lenses
Business Quality, Valuation, Long-Term Structure, Market Timing.

## Long-Term Structure
Independent 5Y history is aggregated to weekly bars. AURYN measures 20/50/200-WMA context, validated multi-year swing range, .382/.50/.618/.786 retracement context, 1.272/1.618 extensions, consolidation duration, structural accumulation range, confirmation, prior high and invalidation. Wave output is explicitly a confidence-scored candidate and secondary evidence, never a deterministic forecast.

## Fundamental Value
Technical targets are never reused as fair value. When valuation is partial, the UI presents available valuation evidence instead of a giant empty placeholder or fabricated Bear/Base/Bull values.

## Analyst thesis
Overview now answers Why Own It, What It's Worth, Where to Accumulate, What Confirms, What Breaks, What Changed and Next Decision.

## UX
Four responsive analyst cards; desktop long-term roadmap rail; non-overlapping mobile level ladder; compact Fib/WMA evidence; analyst thesis grid. Existing chart, daily execution map, catalysts/risks and V24.2 always-on price state remain intact.
