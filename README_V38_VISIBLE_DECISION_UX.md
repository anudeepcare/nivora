# NIVORA V38 — Visible Decision UX

This release deliberately changes the first-glance experience rather than adding another hidden research layer.

## What is visibly different
- New dark Decision Contract hero with action, setup score, conviction, evidence quality, planned R:R and current location.
- Entry / confirm / target / thesis-break plan is one compact price strip.
- WHY THIS CALL is directly beneath the price plan.
- Technical, analyst, institutional and fair-value evidence is compressed into four supporting cards.
- Research-beta/legal disclosure is visible on the decision card with direct links to Why NIVORA, How to use and Terms.
- Time horizon, position status and view controls are restyled as one compact command area.
- Stronger NIVORA branding and mobile-specific layout.

## What did not change
- Price/data APIs
- SEC 13F sync and institutional calculations
- Existing scoring and decision calculations
- Technical/options engines
- Authentication

## Beta positioning
NIVORA is research and decision-support software. It does not guarantee profits. Full Terms and Investment/Data Disclaimer are included in the app and linked from the decision surface.
