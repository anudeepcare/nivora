# AURYN V9.7 — CIO Engine

## What changed
- Added a two-speed CIO engine: Slow Brain for compounder quality and Fast Brain for deployment/timing.
- Long-term quality no longer depends on tactical technical weakness.
- Added corroboration-based business deterioration so one noisy quarter cannot break a thesis.
- New-money, owner and long-term actions are decided separately.
- Relaxed the requirement for perfect technical confirmation before START_SMALL when business quality, evidence and asymmetry are sufficient.
- Preserved hard risk/thesis vetoes.
- Opportunity scoring now weights business quality, forward fundamentals and valuation more heavily than tactical participation/relative-strength noise.
- Decision audit now explicitly reports high-quality WAIT and high-quality AVOID cases.
- Counterfactual upgrade conditions are added to the canonical decision trigger.
- UX was not intentionally modified.

## Research basis
V9.7 synthesizes durable-business / owner-earnings / reinvestment economics, quality/profitability research, expectations-based valuation, trend/momentum as timing, risk/asymmetry and evidence-quality concepts. The implementation does not claim to reproduce any investor's proprietary method.

## Important limitation
V9.7 is a stronger deterministic decision architecture, not a claim of proven excess returns. The next scientific milestone is point-in-time historical calibration across a survivorship-bias-free universe.
