# NIVORA V58 — Long-Term Intelligence + Operational Cleanup

## What changed
- Added explicit 1–3Y Long-Term Thesis output, separate from near-term timing.
- Added an Expectation Gap layer based on forward growth, earnings/revision direction and catalysts; it is not a price target.
- Preserved V57.2 valuation integrity: unavailable absolute fair value remains N/A and technical zones cannot masquerade as intrinsic value.
- GitHub workflows are manual-only (`workflow_dispatch`) to stop unused scheduled/push workflow failures and email noise.
- Added behavioral regression tests for long-term-vs-near-term separation and expectation-gap behavior.

## Interpretation
A company may be long-term constructive while its near-term setup is weak. NIVORA must explain both states instead of allowing daily price action to rewrite the business thesis.

## Next validation layer
The next research release should replay point-in-time historical evidence and benchmark every frozen decision against SPY/sector outcomes before any autonomous live execution is enabled.
