# NIVORA V65.22 — Mobile Component Refinement

- Information icons are smaller, thinner and visually secondary while retaining the same explanation behavior.
- Visual Intelligence no longer shows a meaningless sparse performance chart; Drivers becomes the initial useful view until at least 3 exact snapshots exist.
- Performance remains available automatically when meaningful history exists.
- Portfolio holdings remove duplicated Qty from the identity line, use compact two-column facts and a dedicated NIVORA decision row.
- Edit/delete actions are compact secondary controls.
- New-capital qualification cards use larger readable labels, quieter thresholds and stronger score hierarchy.
- No scoring, market-data, portfolio-math or Trading Lab policy changes.
