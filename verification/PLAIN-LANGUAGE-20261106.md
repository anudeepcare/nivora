# Workspace wording — November 6, 2026

Replaced promotional workspace phrases with feature names, specific actions and descriptions of the underlying information. Public homepage wording is unchanged.

Examples:
- Make your next move count → Compare your companies
- Discover with a purpose → Find companies
- Compare the businesses → Compare companies
- Make the trade-offs visible → Compare growth, margins and valuation
- Find a contract you can understand → Options contracts
- Build a payoff you understand → Compare strategy payoffs
- Pressure-test the decision → Risk & scenarios
- The distance back → Decline from the closing high
- Follow what matters → No alert rules yet
- One change. The whole picture → Review a holding

Scope: twelve workspace components, including current and alternate workspace views. Settings, Alerts and comparison subtitles now describe concrete functions. Calculation methods, data values, warning thresholds, routes and financial assessments were not changed.

Validation: 1,064 regression tests and production build passed. This is a wording update, not certification of the outstanding native iPhone PWA status-bar or scrolling issues. Those remain open as recorded in MOBILE-CHROME-20261106.md.
