# Mock mismatch audit

## Evidence and causes
Reviewed the approved desktop and mobile reference images, the user's original seven screenshots, five dark/help/admin screenshots, and eight later Research/Settings screenshots. The later image labelled mobile in a follow-up was the desktop ServiceNow reference.

The old output was not merely the wrong color: its company masthead and chart tools consumed the first viewport; decision copy and duplicate metrics created tall blocks; short evidence cards stretched to the tallest data category; multiple appended selectors overrode earlier intent. A Technicals compact selector targeted a nonexistent class. A funding selector targeted div while the markup used section. Nested details and their summaries each drew borders. Recovery did not opt into the rebuilt login stylesheet. These concrete causes were corrected in the components that own them rather than by adding a global override.

## Composition
Desktop retains chart + decision, four summary cards, and five primary evidence cards. All overflow metrics remain below rather than disappearing. Mobile has decision-first flow, financial snapshot, compact business strip and remaining metrics. Scenario values, periods and missing-data explanations remain backed by existing logic. Pace bands and labels use actual computed zones. Interaction sapphire is distinct from semantic positive/negative colors. Dark colors and labels were explicitly adjusted rather than inheriting the light palette.

## Preservation and independent review
An independent source review caught lost period basis and source URLs during metric deduplication. Help now retains period basis and all unique source links, covered by rendered component tests. Recovery behavior, provider integrations, trading, billing and financial algorithms are unchanged. The safe-area dock, keyboard targets and reduced-motion/transparency behavior remain.

Detailed per-route and Research-subtab source inventories are in `../workspace-route-audit.md` and `../research-subtab-audit.md`.

## Acceptance boundaries
No browser screenshots of this candidate could be captured: Chromium was prevented from opening sockets, and the cloud browser preview was explicitly blocked. Neither restriction was bypassed. Supplied screenshots and source review are not equivalent to rendering this candidate. No claim of pixel-perfect matching, device performance, every live account state, exact DCA Optimizer agreement or universally available estimates follows from this work.

This is a preproduction source candidate. No production deployment was performed.
