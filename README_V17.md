# NIVORA V17 — Beginner Math + 5-Year Record

Adds a mathematical decision layer without exposing complexity to beginners.

## New
- Business score normalized to 0–100 from SEC financial evidence.
- Up to 5 years of SEC annual revenue / net-income / operating-cash-flow history.
- 5-year record score and plain-English trend.
- 6-month chart score, return, range and drawdown from existing OHLCV data.
- Beginner "If you read only one thing" card: overall score + actual action.
- The overall score blends business quality, 6-month record, current entry quality and risk.
- Existing Now / Swing / Long term / I own it decisions remain the actual action framework.
- Legal foundation pages: Terms, Privacy, Investment & Data Disclaimer.
- No new paid API account required.

## Important
The mathematical score is decision support, not a guarantee. Historical performance does not guarantee future returns.
