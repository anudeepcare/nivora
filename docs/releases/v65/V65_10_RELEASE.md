## Trading Lab
- Five proof stages render as five equal desktop columns: Decisions Checked → Qualified Buy Signals → Risk/Execution → Paper Orders → Completed.

## Thesis consistency
- AI infrastructure is explicitly treated as capital-intensive growth when interpreting financial weakness.
- A low financial score alone no longer automatically creates the generic hard financial veto for AI infrastructure.
- A hard AI-infrastructure financing veto now requires weak financial evidence together with weak forward evidence.
- Capital-intensive financial weakness still penalizes the thesis; it is not ignored.
- A financing-risk-only AI infrastructure case with still-investable growth/forward evidence becomes SPECULATIVE / HIGH RISK + WAIT for new money instead of being automatically labeled AVOID.
- Existing-holder and new-money labels are explicit in the decision UI.

## Safety
- No blanket bullish override for IREN or any ticker.
- Filing/dilution risk, negative FCF + extreme liabilities, weak growth/forward evidence, timing, valuation, quote-integrity and execution risk gates remain active.
