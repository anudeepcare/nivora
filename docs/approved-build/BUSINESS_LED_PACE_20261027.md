# Business first. A clear decision. AURYN Pace.

This complete-source release supersedes the decision-v4 valuation veto while preserving the approved design and all earlier cumulative fixes. The monthly-v5 price calculation is unchanged. Production has not been deployed.

## What changes

| Situation | New purchase | Existing holding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business qualifies, confirmed P1–P4, usable price and financial reports | Buy gradually | Add gradually |
| Business qualifies, confirmed P0 | Wait; pause contributions | Hold; pause additions |
| Measured operating funding pressure or sustained business deterioration | Avoid | Reduce |
| Acute funding pressure: operating burn, under six months of runway, negative equity and debt | Avoid | Sell |
| Essential inputs missing/conflicting, or business criteria not met | Review with the affected check | Review with the affected check |

The profitable-business and funded-growth eligibility screens remain disclosed heuristics. Price weakness does not turn a weak business into a Buy. A growing, qualifying business can support gradual buying even above a conservative generic valuation estimate. Valuation remains visible as a distinct caution; it is not replaced with a fabricated fair value or omitted to manufacture a Buy.

P1 is the baseline scheduled contribution; P2–P4 increase its relative pace within the investor's cash budget and position limit. This is research guidance, not an automated order or a suitable personal allocation. P4 is neither a company-quality rating nor a forecast of fourfold returns. P0 is not Sell. Forming readings do not replace the confirmed period. A daily price change can update the value comparison without rewriting a completed monthly accumulation decision; new business evidence and freshness failures can still change the action.

Without confirmed Pace, the pre-existing independent valuation-entry path remains available when business, value and momentum checks qualify. Otherwise Review identifies the missing contribution history. No Pace level is invented.

The shared decision record feeds Research, Home, holdings and tools. Pace guidance, help, homepage, account screens, About/Pace education, metadata and methodology now explain the same approach. Unused legacy Hold/Wait slogans were removed. The approved layouts and raw price calculation were preserved.

## Limits and release scope

Policy tests establish implementation consistency, not investment outperformance. Historical reports in this repository do not validate decision-v5 or the current monthly-v5 revision. No superiority, profitability or universal data-coverage claim is made. The business screen remains a limited rules-based assessment rather than a complete assessment of a company's future prospects. Existing data licensing and commercial/legal launch review requirements remain unchanged.

No new dependency, database migration or environment variable is required. Temporary responsive-QA files and preview configuration are excluded from the delivered source. The ZIP includes the full cumulative application and a verified SHA-256 manifest.
