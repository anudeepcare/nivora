# Auryn user guide

## A simple daily workflow

1. **Home:** review the companies you follow, upcoming reports and recent context.
2. **Research:** open a company, read the investment decision and its qualifications, then inspect the business, valuation and risks.
3. **Watchlist:** save ideas you want to revisit. A saved company is not a buy instruction.
4. **Portfolio:** keep recorded positions and cash accurate, review concentration and compare observations with the same basis.

## Understand the numbers

- **Quote:** use its timestamp and status. Delayed data is not a live execution price.
- **Pace:** a price-based accumulation framework. A valid level requires the right history and freshness. Missing coverage is not P0, and business checks can disagree with the price zone.
- **Bear / base / bull:** conditional outcomes, not guarantees. Analyst low/consensus/high are different from Auryn's own modeled scenarios; keep their labels and horizons separate.
- **History:** daily, weekly and monthly observations are not interchangeable. Coarser histories do not recreate absent daily sessions.
- **Financials:** compare the same currency, period and accounting basis. Unavailable is not zero.
- **Ownership:** insider, institutional and congressional disclosures are delayed records. They are not proof of intent, timing or a recommendation.
- **Options:** aggregate activity and volatility describe context. They are not a full tradable chain or evidence of who bought or sold.
- **News:** publisher links lead to the source. A sentiment score or generated narrative is not the original article or a verified investment conclusion.

## The interface

Primary figures stay on screen. Explanation buttons open a dialog so a card does not expand and push the rest of the page around. Close with the close button or Escape; focus returns to the button that opened it. Tables may scroll horizontally on smaller screens without changing their values.

Use Settings for appearance and available preferences. The installed iPhone app uses the same account as the browser. Device theme and reduced-transparency preferences should not make data unreadable.

## Data coverage during migration

SentiSense-only mode does not silently fall back to another provider. A data type that the selected feed cannot supply must be explicitly unavailable. In particular, delayed quotes, coarse long-term history and aggregate options must not be relabeled as live quotes, daily history or a complete option chain. See the migration audit for implementation coverage and release gates.
