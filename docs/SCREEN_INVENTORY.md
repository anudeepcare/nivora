# Current Fluid experience screen inventory

- Public landing/sample: compact graphical canvas, mobile navigation, account/pricing links and footer.
- Login/signup/recovery: social/email actions, confirmation/error/reset states and legal links.
- Home: distinct first-use, watchlist and holdings paths; greeting, session contributors, exposure, calendar, company focus, full matching news and saved companies.
- Research shell: listing/quote context, financial metrics, more market context, save action, keyboard tabs, detail mode, loading/error states.
- Overview: compact assessment and Pace, four grouped evidence areas (operating metrics/charts, price scenarios/multiples, technical chart/studies, ownership/events), Crosscheck, risk, holder context, metric help, checkpoints and analyst methodology.
- Business: history, two-sided thesis, compact profile, driver questions, full evidence lenses and watch conditions.
- Financials: annual/TTM/quarterly; chart explorer and periods; reconciled cash bridge; statements, full metrics and funding context.
- Value: scenarios with shared scale, assumptions, multiples/yields, optional personal workbench and model-fit detail.
- Technicals: daily/weekly/monthly charts, trend/momentum/volatility/participation, formula disclosures, price levels/custom anchors and monthly policy history.
- Analysts/News: targets, recommendation distribution, source dates, publishers, filings, events/history, reported activity and on-demand congressional disclosures.
- Advanced: Risk, Position design, Expectations, Market context and Historical lens; retained tools/charts/forms.
- Options: Market map, Contracts, Long-term, Strategy builder and Understand options, including missing-chain/entitlement states.
- Watchlist: add/filter/remove/refresh, responsive table, quote timestamps/daily change and stock/crypto navigation.
- Portfolio: equity/cash/margin/managed values; Holdings/Allocation/Risk views; filter/sort/weights, allocation charts, sectors, stress tools and position/balance editors.
- Settings: appearance/accent/density/text/motion, research preferences, account/security/providers/password, signout, plans/alerts/help.
- Plans/owner: current membership, free/Pro cards, checkout/manage, owner grants/expiry/access and support filtering.
- Help/contact/legal: reading layout, support form and saved/email/error states, methodology/privacy/terms/risk/reliability.
- Shared: buttons, selects, search/explanation dialogs, focus/reduced-motion/light-dark and responsive shell.

Coverage: verification/approved-experience-browser-report.json, approved-experience-interactions-final.json and fluid-depth-browser.json. This inventory is not a claim that every permission-gated state was live-tested. Physical installed iPhone and real account/provider/payment/email acceptance remain external checks. Mock figures are illustrative; live data is never substituted with them.
