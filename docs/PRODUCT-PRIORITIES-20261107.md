# AURYN product additions — consolidated proposals

Owner request: incorporate the external review and feature ideas. This is an implementation backlog with evidence requirements, not a claim that every feature is shipped. Prioritize a coherent monthly-investor experience over adding more screens. Keep the one-app research and options scope; do not remove existing tools merely because the review prefers another positioning.

| Priority | Addition | Current basis and remaining work | Acceptance rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask why / evidence receipt | Metric help, sources and private handbook exist; add a consistent public receipt for selected metrics | Value, units, period, observation time, source, formula summary and missing inputs agree with the displayed result; private handbook stays private |
| 1 | Thesis checks | Saved thesis and earnings review exist; add explicit user-confirmed measurable conditions | Alert on a sourced change in a specified condition; never infer free-text intent silently |
| 1 | Earnings-season brief | Earnings preparation/review exist; connect before/after evidence and prior guidance | Reported facts and expectations separated; exact source passages and dates |
| 1 | Verified signal record | Checkpoint signing/evaluation machinery exists; public prospective publication not built | Publish immutable dated policy inputs before outcomes, with independent timestamp and correction history; include losses |
| 1 | Confidence and gaps | Coverage/status already present; unify the explanation across tabs | Missing is not zero; stale source and conflicting periods remain visible |
| 2 | Contribution plan | Existing Pace/planning is research-only; explicit monthly budget allocation needs product and suitability boundaries | Cash/concentration caps, every allocation explained, no automatic real orders |
| 2 | Filing changes | Filing evidence exists; exact passage-to-passage diff not complete | Compare correct prior filing and section, show added/deleted text and links; no unsupported tone diagnosis |
| 2 | Portfolio X-ray | Sector concentration and stress views exist; ETF holdings look-through is missing | Dated constituent weights, cross-fund overlap, cash and unknown exposure reconciled to total |
| 2 | Bear-case review | Risk evidence exists; add a focused opposing-case workflow | Every material assertion cited; distinguish contradiction from missing evidence |
| 2 | Shareable research cards | Needs privacy-safe export/share workflow | No account holdings or private thesis leaked; date, model and source retained; no implied recommendation from color |
| 2 | Plain-language presentation | Simple/detailed settings and options Guide exist; audit all screens | Same numbers and gates in both modes, progressively disclosed jargon |
| 2 | Subscription-value recap | Needs event/change history plus opt-in delivery | List verifiable events observed, not invented “losses avoided” or cherry-picked wins |
| 3 | Free portfolio check | Needs anonymous import processing and conversion flow | Clearly label coverage; avoid retaining uploaded holdings without consent |
| 3 | Cited weekly watchlist email | Needs preferences, delivery setup and scheduled aggregation | Opt-in, unsubscribe, deduplicated dated changes, no delivery claim without receipt |

| 3 | Earnings-call comparison | Requires licensed transcripts/timecodes | Quotes trace to original passages; dropped wording is not automatically a broken commitment |
| 3 | Read-only broker sync | Requires chosen provider, consent and credentials | Reconcile holdings/transactions; revoke access; idempotent ingestion; no trading scope by default |
| 3 | Household/shared accounts | Not implemented | Explicit invitations, per-portfolio roles, RLS isolation, revocation and audit |
| 4 | Advisor/creator reports | Needs tenant/branding/export roles | Client isolation and source licensing; no client data in public shares |
| 4 | Data/API tier | Requires redistribution rights and service-level design | Versioned schema, lineage, quotas, key rotation and permitted redistribution |
| 4 | Broker partnerships | Commercial agreement and review needed | Transparent disclosures, consent, separate execution authorization |

| On hold | Decision journal / behavior coaching | Owner previously requested removing this interface | Do not reintroduce without explicit owner direction; never claim a sale was wrong from future price alone |

Suggested positioning to test: “Understand the business. See what changed. Invest at your pace.” This is a product hypothesis, not a claim of exclusive capability or proven conversion uplift. Personalized real-money execution, advisor offerings and commercial redistribution need appropriate professional/provider review before launch.
