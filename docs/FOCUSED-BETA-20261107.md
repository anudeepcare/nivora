# Focused beta: evidence to collect now

Core user: an investor contributing monthly to US-listed stocks. Core job: prepare a contribution with reasons they can revisit. Current worksheet is explicitly USD and user-selected; other markets are not silently treated as supported.

Recruit 20 consenting monthly investors through the owner's existing contacts. No invitations have been sent by this update. Track consent and contact details outside public source.

Observe each person without coaching: find a company; explain its business and a source; write their thesis and what would change their mind; produce a monthly research draft; locate the Pace evidence status. Ask what they misunderstood, what they would use next month, and what they currently pay for. Record completion, time, wrong assumptions and abandoned tasks. Do not count staff or automated tests as users.

Use five observed sessions to identify first fixes, then repeat with new users. Decide whether the planner or thesis review carries the strongest value. Avoid adding unrelated features while these tasks remain confusing.

Before analytics: document consent, event names and retention; never send thesis text, holdings, email addresses or entered portfolio values as event properties. Candidate events: first_research_opened, thesis_saved, research_draft_downloaded, evidence_opened. An event count is not proof of investment results or user satisfaction.

Release gates: no blocked core task on mobile; no known critical security defect; correct owner/member separation; no hidden performance claims; no production payments or execution based solely on a synthetic test. Legal review and actual customer research require the relevant people.
