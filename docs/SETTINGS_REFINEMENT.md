# Settings refinement

The full settings page and quick settings sheet now share theme, text-size, reduced-motion, accent and spacing controls. Appearance comes first in the sheet; account identity and sign-out remain clear on the full page. Research preferences stay collapsed. Help and legal links are compact. Password-update feedback is visible outside the collapsed password section.

Scoped styling supports narrow screens and both themes without modifying the PWA header. Existing preference storage and authentication behavior are preserved. Verification: verification/settings-cleanup-verify.log. Physical iPhone behavior and live account changes were not tested.
