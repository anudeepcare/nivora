# Evidence-led AURYN build
Approved scope: public mobile-first storefront; readable answer/evidence/depth research; versioned business and valuation suitability; original monthly accumulation candidate; equal-budget chronological evaluation; connected portfolio risk; preserve PWA and pricing.

Implementation boundaries: existing production monthly-v1 remains the default. Add transparent robust monthly-v2 as an experimental candidate, not a proven replacement. Do not infer competitor rules, publish success probabilities, use future fundamentals in historical tests, or invent specialist fair values. Corporate models stay separate from crypto and unsupported sectors. Use existing subscriptions and data already loaded. No live orders, database writes, deployment or new paid dependencies.

Candidate specification: 36 consecutive settled month-end closes; robust Theil–Sen regression on log prices, median-centred residuals and MAD scale with 3% log-price floor. Drawdown guards 15/30/45% plus robust z <= -.75/-1.5/-2 define 2/3/4x. Pause when z >=1.5 and price >=20% above fitted trend. A separate z >=2.5 and 12-month gain >=50% triggers trim review; never an automatic sale. Forming observations cannot rewrite confirmed history. Explicit price thresholds are research conditions, not fair value.

Evaluation: identical monthly 100-unit deposits, carried unspent cash, no borrowing, prior completed signal and next month's first observed open. Fees/slippage 0–200bps. Compare normal DCA, monthly-v1 and candidate-v2 over identical eligible months. Final 30% of eligible months is a chronological holdout with fresh accounts; model is not tuned on it. Report cost, cash, drawdown, terminal value and failed/insufficient history. Frozen rules are not evidence of future profitability. Synthetic fixtures validate mechanics only.

Public: dedicated root storefront and /sample walkthrough, separate /login signup/recovery. Public walkthrough is explicitly illustrative, with no provider calls or invented live results. Existing localhost development preview stays restricted. Signed-in root visitors may enter /overview. Respect mobile safe areas and reduced motion. Isolated public CSS must not alter product shell.

Research: one concise business/value/accumulation/risk summary, dated evidence and suitability audit. Reuse existing decision, outlook and financial metrics rather than duplicate scores. Existing technicals includes original version; candidate and evaluation are opt-in depth below its chart. Add coloured monthly candles with labels and accessible legend. No contribution amount form is needed.
