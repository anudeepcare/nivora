# AURYN V9.6 Theme + Technicals Consolidation

## Goal
Replace accumulated theme overrides with one semantic paint contract and make Technicals a calm, premium analytical surface without changing AURYN decision logic, charts, market truth, or approved Research hero geometry.

## Locked UX
- Research hero/chart geometry and navigation remain unchanged.
- Market Truth, engine decisions, providers, portfolio calculations, chart levels, and canonical evidence remain unchanged.
- Themes change paint/readability only.

## Theme contract
Seven user-facing editions remain: Classic, Noir, Sapphire, Racing Green, Bordeaux, Arctic, Bronze.
All editions use a calm readable editorial canvas; edition identity is expressed through chrome, hero, accents, borders, and subtle page tint. Dense reading surfaces remain high-contrast.
Every painted surface owns its foreground. Financial positive/negative/warning semantics do not change by theme.

## Technicals
Technicals has four visual levels:
1. Market State strip
2. Technical Verdict
3. Six factor cards
4. Indicator Board
No giant gray wrapper, alternating dark cells, or theme-dependent white-on-white text.

## Readability
Standard is the default. Large must visibly increase supporting copy, labels, tabs, metrics and footer copy while preserving layout.

## Coverage
Theme contract explicitly covers global chrome, stock masthead, evidence navigation, every stock tab, portfolio, settings, forms/search, charts, Technicals, auth, dialogs, and ProductFooter.
