# AURYN Investment Roadmap Design

## Goal
Extend Research from three tactical pillars into a decision-grade long-term investment system without weakening canonical price/execution truth.

## Four primary lenses
1. Business: quality, trajectory, financial durability, forward evidence.
2. Valuation: method-appropriate fundamental evidence and scenarios; never reuse technical targets as fair value.
3. Long-Term Structure: weekly multi-year trend, WMA, validated swing/Fibonacci zones, consolidation, relative/participation evidence, and optional wave candidate with confidence/invalidation.
4. Timing: existing daily/4H execution structure and entry quality.

## Decision narrative
Every qualified stock should answer: Why own it? What is it worth? Where to accumulate? What confirms? What breaks? What changed? What is the next decision trigger?

## Truth constraints
- Missing evidence is missing, never zero.
- Bear/Base/Bull values publish only from decision-grade fundamental evidence.
- Fib levels are structural zones, not guaranteed forecasts.
- Wave labels are candidates with confidence and alternate/invalidation context, never facts.
- Fundamental thesis break and technical invalidation remain separate.
- Long-term structure cannot silently rewrite new-money execution.
- Existing Market Truth and V24.2 price state remain unchanged.

## UX
Desktop: four compact pillar cards, then Fundamental Value and Long-Term Roadmap side-by-side, then thesis/action panels.
Mobile: single-column cards; roadmap becomes a non-overlapping level ladder rather than compressed labels.
Charts use compact sparklines/mini-bars for 5-year business trends and a weekly roadmap chart for structure.
