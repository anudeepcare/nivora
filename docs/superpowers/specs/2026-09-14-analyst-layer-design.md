# AURYN Analyst Layer Design

Approved visual target: the PANW analyst mockup from the September 14 session.

## Contract
Keep the current AURYN Call, canonical Market Truth, CIO decision semantics, chart and price structure. Add a simple analyst layer directly below the first-screen decision:
1. COMPANY — slow clock. Business quality and trajectory from fundamental/forward evidence only.
2. VALUE — medium clock. Valuation attractiveness from the canonical valuation pillar and independent fundamental scenario evidence when available.
3. TIMING — fast clock. Entry/market setup from canonical entry quality, market structure and confirmed technical evidence.

Scenario Spectrum is fundamental/valuation context when independent fundamental scenarios exist; otherwise it must explicitly say that independent fundamental valuation is unavailable rather than pretending technical targets are fair value.

Decision Map remains execution-only and uses canonical structural levels. Exact x-coordinates are preserved; labels may move into collision lanes but their nodes do not.

Below the clocks, present compact Key Catalysts, Key Risks, What's Changed and AURYN View. Only use evidence already present in the canonical snapshot/current research payload. Missing evidence displays unavailable/insufficient evidence. No invented company facts.

Desktop follows the approved premium three-column composition. Mobile becomes a deliberate one-column/compact two-column composition with readable text and no horizontal breakage.

No changes to Market Truth, provider/session authority, portfolio calculations, autonomous jobs or CIO thresholds.
