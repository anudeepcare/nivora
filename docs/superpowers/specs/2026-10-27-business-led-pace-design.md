# Business-led AURYN Pace

User-approved objective: useful Buy/Add/Hold/Reduce/Sell guidance; business progress must not be confused with share-price movement. P4 is a contribution level, not a quality score. Public, account and research pages must explain the same model without changing the approved layout.

## Decision contract

1. Current comparable financials and a usable price are required. Missing or conflicting inputs produce Review, explicitly distinct from an adverse investment conclusion.
2. Acute funding pressure produces Sell for holdings / Avoid for new purchases. Funding pressure or sustained operating deterioration produces Reduce / Avoid. Existing measurable thresholds remain unchanged.
3. A supported profitable business or qualifying funded-growth business plus confirmed P1–P4 supports Buy / Add gradually. Pace governs the relative scheduled contribution, within the investor's budget and position limit. It does not promise returns or prescribe an unlimited investment.
4. Confirmed P0 produces Wait for new purchases / Hold for holdings and pauses additions. P0 is not a Sell signal. Forming readings never replace confirmed readings.
5. Valuation sensitivities and short-term momentum become explicit cautions for the confirmed accumulation path, not universal purchase vetoes. A conservative cash-flow estimate is not a complete intrinsic-value verdict, particularly for reinvesting growth companies.
6. If confirmed Pace is unavailable, preserve the existing independent valuation-entry path when its business, value and momentum tests pass; otherwise Review. No fabricated Pace.
7. Business criteria that are not met produce Review unless stronger measured risks justify Avoid/Reduce/Sell. Review must identify the missing or failed criteria, not use generic "wait for a better price" copy.

## Boundaries

Keep monthly-v5 calculation, share-denominator integrity, financial freshness, actual earnings handling, approved layout, and all cumulative source changes. No fabricated coverage, performance claims, live test recommendations, production publication, or forced distribution of Buy labels. Label independent valuation references as model screens rather than mandatory entry prices for accumulation. Code tests establish policy consistency, not investment outperformance.
