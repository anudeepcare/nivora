## Global Constraints
- Version 1.0.0 retained. No deploy, live database mutation, subscriptions, or invented market evidence.
- Preserve current changes, no broad refactor. Desktop/mobile, dark/light and accessible controls required.
- Model agreement is not evidence of performance; completed and forming periods remain distinct.

## Review Focus
- Incomplete history and stale reference prices must not produce current trade claims.
- Negative EPS and mismatched fiscal periods must not generate misleading growth.
- Partial scan denominators must not be called whole-market coverage.
- Historical event samples must exclude future/incomplete returns and identify overlapping observations.
- Small-screen overflow, long labels and dense real-data panels must render correctly.
