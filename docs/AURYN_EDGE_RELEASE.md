# AURYN Edge — preproduction review build

This release implements the approved research hierarchy and retains the complete application source. It has not been pushed or deployed. Production and live accounts are unchanged.

## Visible changes
- AURYN Edge first, with Staying Power, Price Advantage and Entry Conditions.
- Confirmed AURYN Pace is primary. Forming conditions remain provisional. Financial conflict or severe risk can pause contributions independently of the price zone.
- Simple mode keeps the calculation explanation collapsed; Detailed mode opens it.
- Concrete financial metrics and accessible label explanations; positive share-count growth uses caution, not a success color.
- One scenario scale, existing conditional earnings sensitivities or distinctly labelled historical price-risk cases; analyst consensus is separate.
- No repeated financial/lens/threshold panels below Overview. Full evidence and threshold tools remain in Business details.
- Revenue-by-business/region controls and their requests removed.
- Home uses a compact evidence pulse instead of the large decorative hero. Counts apply to the visibly identified company group; missing data does not become a zero count.
- Landing and account copy/layout refined within the approved visual system; header uses company name and reported growth/cash-generation context.
- Contact is public, with persistent request references and an owner-only Support section.
- Chart locale initialization, SVG scenario-title hydration and the preview resource render loop corrected.

## Evidence rules
Edge v1 is an experimental, disclosed decision policy, not a calibrated probability, proprietary competitor clone, intrinsic value estimate, order instruction or proven outperformance model. Existing sector models, quote adapters and financial-period checks remain in place.

Build requires positive reported cash generation and operating margin, nonnegative comparable revenue growth, supported conditional scenario gates, constructive trend, confirmed Pace at least 2, a usable price and no severe/conflicting evidence. A labelled settled close may remain a research reference for up to four days when it matches the same-date daily bar; it is explicitly not a live execution price. These gates are testable policy choices, not optimized thresholds. A missing or extreme valuation sensitivity never becomes a favorable entry reading. Financial/insurance/REIT-style businesses require sector review rather than inheriting a corporate cash-flow verdict. Negative investment cash flow alone does not label a growing company weak.

Confirmed price Pace and evidence-adjusted Pace are explicitly distinguished. Existing position ownership may change Wait to Hold & monitor when operating evidence is supported and price conditions limit additions; it does not establish personal suitability.

## Remaining acceptance boundaries
- No physical installed iPhone/Safari acceptance was performed. Automated mobile viewport checks are not a replacement for this.
- No live provider credentials, 30–50 ticker competitor comparison, numerical DCA parity or independently validated all-ticker valuation is claimed.
- Source data and illustrative fixtures differ from mockup numbers. Unsupported inputs remain unavailable.
- Live email, Google/X production configuration, billing and payment processing were not exercised. No real enquiries/emails/orders/payments were generated.
- Screenshot-level zero deviation is not established by a generated image; inspect the running preprod UI before promotion.
