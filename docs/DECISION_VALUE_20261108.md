# Decision context and Options alignment — November 8, 2026

Holdings and watchlists now expose the existing decision record through a Review first section: assessment, measured reason, risk and reassessment condition, plus distinct price-reference and financial-period dates. Rows are prioritized for review, not expected returns. Missing or paused evidence cannot become Buy. A holding-only record cannot recommend a new purchase. No extra research fetch is introduced.

Position-size attention uses the user's existing saved concentration threshold only. It preserves the business action and labels its denominator as priced investments excluding cash and unpriced holdings. It does not automatically create Sell advice or an order.

Options uses explicit command/header grids, consistent filter heights and exposure-summary value baselines. Populated fixture checks cover light/dark and 320/390/768/1440 widths, including practice navigation.

Bear/base/bull remain assumption-based scenarios. Earnings cases typically combine low/central/high annual EPS and 80%/100%/120% multiples. Price-anchored cases are sensitivities, not independently established fair value or calibrated outcome probabilities. Neither the sorting nor the layout claims to improve forecast hit rate. Forecast accuracy requires a dated, out-of-sample outcome record; no numerical accuracy claim is made here.

No production deployment, trades, database changes or live-provider performance benchmark were performed.
