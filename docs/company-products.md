Official contract: https://sentisense.ai/docs/api/stocks/ (checked 2026-11-18). One fixed GET /api/v1/stocks/{ticker}/entities on explicit Load linked products. Bare array, at most1000 input rows within2MB; exact relatedStock ticker on every row, only PRODUCT entries projected with valid unique public slug. Null handles are counted as omitted, not inferred from internal IDs. At most50 product matches shown with explicit truncation; no unsupported provider limit parameter. Other entity types are not product matches. appId marks tracked iOS app metadata only; it does not prove review history exists. No ownership, adoption, revenue, complete catalog, ranking, or observation-date inference. Mismatched associated ticker/invalid shape/preview are withheld, not converted to full coverage.

Selected product enters existing exact PRODUCT/slug-verified detail loader; app history remains separately opt-in. Name lookup remains available. Discovery requests have no metrics fanout, and private profile/person fields are not projected. Cached mounted results may be older; refresh is explicit and retrieval time is not observation time.
