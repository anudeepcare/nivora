# Market trackers workspace

Official contract reviewed 2026-11-18: https://sentisense.ai/docs/api/trackers/ . Screenshot navigation is a reference, not a claim that every provider feature is implemented.

Screener `/options` retains Stock screener and Market options, and adds a lazy Market trackers workspace. Its catalog requests `/api/v1/trackers` once per resource cache lifetime. A user selection requests only that fixed allowlisted tracker; no detail fan-out or polling. All eleven currently documented IDs are eligible. Future catalog IDs are omitted pending review. Unknown schema or incompatible metric structures are unavailable, not flattened into invented values. No live provider calls were made during implementation.

Transport: server-only, fixed HTTPS origin, no redirects, 12-second timeout, streaming 2 MB maximum; sanitized errors. Route requires authenticated market account, non-sample deployment, overview AND advanced features, per-user six/minute limit and research reservation. Additional shared tracker-only20/minute limit is NOT a unified budget across all SentiSense adapters. Catalog metadata does not grant app or provider entitlement. PRO previews retain preview state and withheld layer names. App plan gating may be narrower than provider Free access.

Projection retains closed primitive metrics, units, nulls versus zero, data-as-of labels, metric periods, flags, source links and methodology notes. `asOf` is provider free-form date/quarter, not a parsed realtime timestamp. Returned rows locally page25 at a time. Full response count stays visible. Quotes are delayed. Heatmap uses an explicitly labeled price-only table, not an area-scaled treemap; sentiment/options overlays and sector rollups are not exposed in this initial renderer. No score is rebranded as an Auryn prediction. No estimate becomes a trade recommendation.

Limitations: live entitlement and payload compatibility remain unverified; a new schema is fail-closed. No automatic retries. No new purchase/settings. Existing stock screener retains its documented six filters; screenshot presets requiring broader score, multiple-condition, or technical definitions are not copied or claimed implemented. Earnings calendar remains in existing Events rather than claiming a new global calendar under this slice. No assertion of commercial display rights beyond the user's applicable provider terms; licensing is not conferred by the API working.
