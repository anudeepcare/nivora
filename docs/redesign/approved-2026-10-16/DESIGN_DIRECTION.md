Show missing evidence clearly without filling the page with repeated unavailable boxes. Preserve the canonical decisions and all existing evidence gates.

## Visual rules

- Mobile first at 320, 375, 390 and 430px; desktop at 1280, 1440 and 1920px. Preserve keyboard access and zoom.
- Ink navy, electric blue, cool white surfaces, restrained positive teal, amber for caution and red for negative values. Dark theme uses midnight navy.
- Glass on sticky chrome, tabs and overlays; opaque, high-contrast surfaces beneath financial data. Subtle shadows, 20–24px major-card radii, 12–16px internal tiles. No decorative gradient charts or arbitrary score rings.
- Shared spacing scale 4/8/12/16/24/32px. Stable numeric columns and tabular figures. Chart gutters and card titles align. Short labels, no paragraph walls.
- Data visualizations derive only from available comparable data. Never invent trends, moat scores, valuation values, live prices or financial claims to match mockups.
- Home uses one search and user-owned recent/saved research. No hardcoded recommendation cards or duplicated main menu. Stock pages retain compact search and a concise tab system.
- All metrics have tap/keyboard-accessible explanations. Keep source dates and uncertainty close to values, and detailed provenance expandable.
- Readable mobile text and 44px primary touch targets; no hover-only help; safe-area padding; reduced-motion support; restrained blur to limit GPU cost.
