# AURYN Decision Canvas specification

Approved intent: actual code, preserving functionality, using the latest calm white/cobalt desktop and mobile mocks. Generated images are visual references, not financial data or a literal claim of validated performance.

Global constraints
- Work locally on auryn-decision-canvas-preprod; never deploy or push main.
- Preserve PWA shell geometry, safe areas, session ownership, prices, billing and existing features.
- Keep useful conclusions available when another calculation lacks evidence. Never fabricate data or hide material uncertainty.
- Price Pace completed/developing must be separate from business risk; no overwritten price multiplier.
- Shared assessment follows answer, supporting facts, limiting risk, next checkpoint. Standard metric names retained.
- Authenticated Home has first-use, watchlist and holdings compositions; preserve news, calendar, contributors, market context and followed companies.
- Current Free100/Pro unlimited entitlements remain unchanged.
- Dark/light, mobile-first with reduced motion and accessible controls across every route.
- Contact must not prefill/expose personal email.
- No paid subscriptions, email delivery, database migrations, trading or live orders activated.

Scope
1 Decision logic and Pace corrections, ownership-aware assessments, explanatory partial states.
2 Shared component design tokens, typography, controls, contextual help and branded Pace/Checkpoints.
3 Overview rebuild with decision-first hierarchy, valid independent conclusions, scenarios and charts preserved.
4 Public/auth/account and support refinement preserving real flows.
5 Home adaptive compositions and evidence/source relevance.
6 Research tabs consistent technical depth and help, existing functionality preserved; no phantom data.
7 Portfolio/watchlist/settings refined consistently via shared system, no feature deletion.
8 Full tests/build/API/browser light/dark/mobile verification, independent review, full-source ZIP and preprod guide.

Broader research roadmap not silently claimed implemented: cited filing AI, semantic moat extraction, automated journals/digests/conditional alert scheduling, complete historical backtests and congressional ingestion require separate actual implementations/data verification. Existing capabilities remain accessible.
