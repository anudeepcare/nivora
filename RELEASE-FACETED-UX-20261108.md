# AURYN — Faceted Pace and compact workspace

This release supersedes the flat presentation in the earlier decision-first handoff.

## Product changes

- AURYN Pace uses layered teal/emerald gradients, a large faceted diamond watermark and its persistent diamond stage scale. The primary decision and its short reason remain prominent. All nine actions are retained.
- Stocks without sufficient monthly history keep a neutral five-stage framework. No active price zone or threshold is invented. Repeated unavailable-history messages and empty date labels are removed.
- Expanded price bands use readable inherited text and a subtle current-row highlight; unrelated last-row styling cannot turn the P4 row into white text on a pale background.
- Home uses a single comparison hero instead of two large competing banners. The comparison title and description have explicit high-contrast colors. Unused generic-banner CSS is removed.
- News starts with six balanced highlights, retains every remaining update, and omits redundant research links because each company name already opens research.
- Missing cached sparklines offer a direct chart link, respecting preview routes, instead of passive unloaded-history text. No additional research-request fan-out is introduced.
- Targets no longer duplicates the quote header box. Existing model inputs, forecasts, metrics and Pro tools remain available.
- Customer-visible contribution terminology is updated throughout the application and owner guide. Internal identifiers, compatibility keys and test inputs remain intact.

## Calculation scope

No Pace classification, scenario formula or price-band algorithm was changed. The saved comparison remains 159/182 agreement on the reserved stocks (87.4%) and 53/69 in the reserved deeper categories (76.8%). These are category-agreement figures on a saved reference snapshot, not investment-return or future-price forecast accuracy.

Source-only delivery. No deployment or database change was performed.
