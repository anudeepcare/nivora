# NIVORA V48 — Thesis-First Investor Engine

## North star
NIVORA studies the business first, the future second, valuation/expectations third, risk fourth, and price action last. Price can change opportunity. Price alone cannot rewrite company quality or the long-term thesis.

## Three separate decisions
1. **Company score** — slow-moving business quality: financial strength, profitability, growth durability and multi-year evidence.
2. **Thesis score** — 6–36 month forward investment case: company quality, growth, earnings execution, analysts, reported institutions and catalysts.
3. **Opportunity score** — whether current price is attractive enough for new capital: thesis + valuation + price dislocation + risk + a small technical-confirmation layer.

This prevents a 10% daily decline from automatically converting a strong business into an AVOID.

## Decision vocabulary
New capital: STRONG BUY OPPORTUNITY / ACCUMULATE / WATCH / AVOID.
Existing owner: HOLD / ACCUMULATE / REDUCE / EXIT-REASSESS.

Actions are decision-support outputs, not guaranteed outcomes or automated trade instructions.

## Evidence hierarchy
- SEC/company fundamentals and multi-year business record
- Forward growth and earnings execution
- Analyst consensus/revisions and verified targets when available
- Reported institutional/insider evidence with explicit filing delay
- Company catalysts/news
- Valuation evidence
- Market regime, trend, momentum and flow as risk/confirmation inputs only

Missing evidence lowers confidence; it is never silently interpreted as bearish.

## Today
Today is a **material-change feed**, not a daily trader list. It surfaces changes in thesis score, thesis state, valuation/opportunity or investor action. A price move alone is not enough.

## Discover
Discover is a persistent investment-universe ranking. A dedicated background process gradually scores eligible U.S. companies using business/forward/valuation evidence. During warm-up it is labelled a preview; it never substitutes alphabetical partial scanner batches.

## Audit / validation
Every decision snapshot is frozen with its evidence. Future releases should calculate 30d / 90d / 180d / 1y / 2y forward outcomes, benchmark-relative results, calibration by score bucket and drawdown. Model weights must eventually be adjusted from forward evidence rather than intuition alone.

## Future execution / bot
Execution is intentionally separate from research. A later broker adapter should only consume explicit, versioned NIVORA decisions after risk controls: max position size, max portfolio loss, liquidity checks, stale-data veto, order-price protections, kill switch, human approval mode, paper-trading period and immutable audit log. V48 contains no automatic trading.
