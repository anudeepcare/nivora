# AURYN V9.6 — Theme + Technicals Consolidation

## What changed
- Replaced the accumulated V9.5.x theme override stack with one semantic theme stylesheet.
- Kept seven simple editions: Classic, Noir, Sapphire, Racing Green, Bordeaux, Arctic, Bronze.
- Dark-named editions now use calm tinted editorial canvases with dark readable ink; identity remains in chrome, hero, accents and borders.
- Added explicit surface/foreground ownership for stock masthead, evidence navigation, all stock tabs, Portfolio, auth/forms, charts, settings and ProductFooter.
- Rebuilt Technicals presentation into four visual levels: Market State, Technical Verdict, six factor cards and responsive Indicator Board.
- Removed the giant gray Technicals wrapper and alternating dark score cells.
- Large readability now scales supporting copy, labels, navigation and footer copy meaningfully.
- Preserved PWA search architecture and appearance persistence.

## Locked / unchanged
Market Truth, providers, decision engine, canonical evidence, portfolio calculations, chart levels, Research hero geometry and navigation behavior were not changed.
