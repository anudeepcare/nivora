# AURYN product and design preferences

Explicit user requirements, recorded November 8, 2026:

- Do not build repeated outlined cards, boxes with colored top borders, or nested bordered panels. This is a repeatedly rejected design pattern.
- Use open layouts, typography, whitespace and meaningful charts. Bear/base/bull retain distinct colors without outlined tiles.
- Pace must lead with the actual decision: Buy, Add, Hold, Wait, Watch, Review, Reduce, Sell or Avoid, using the existing decision engine and business checks. A technical zone number is supporting information, never the headline substitute for a decision.
- No redundant giant P0–P4 badge beside a P1 heading. Price stages remain a compact, borderless diamond scale with actual prices; all precise bands remain accessible.
- Bear/base/bull must remain visible and synchronized. Do not remove scenarios to simplify the screen. Do not change their calculations merely to make numbers look attractive.
- Retain Pro metrics and tools. Simplify presentation instead of deleting useful capabilities.
- Verify the actual visual hierarchy and card borders in browser previews; passing unit tests alone does not validate UX.

- Modern visual depth is required: use visible layered gradients, the faceted AURYN diamond and purposeful graphic treatment. Do not interpret “no outlined boxes” as a request for flat, plain green rectangles or empty unstyled layouts.
- Short-history stocks must keep their decision visible and show a neutral diamond framework without fabricated price levels. Avoid repeated unavailable-data messages.
- Remove visible DCA or DCA Optimizer wording from the app; internal compatibility identifiers and historical test inputs may remain. Use AURYN Pace and plain contribution language.

- Treat UX as one coherent product across Home, Research, Watchlist, Portfolio, Options and the owner guide. Avoid oversized standalone evidence crystals in the Overview. Keep the decision beside the real chart on desktop and visible scenarios directly below; meaningful score detail stays accessible.
